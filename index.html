About Us

The Gingerbread Factory is run by three co-founders: Ariffin, Cactus and Julian. We started with one batch of gingerbread and a lot of opinions about how thick a cookie should be.

We still make everything by hand. The shapes are cut one at a time and the icing is piped by us, so no two cookies are exactly alike. We think that’s part of the charm.

Whether you want a small snail to go with your tea or a big moose to share, we hope you enjoy eating them as much as we enjoy baking them.
